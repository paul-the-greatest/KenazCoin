blockchain.py
-------------
A chain of Block objects linked by their hashes.
 
Key responsibilities:
  - Create the genesis block (the very first block, with no predecessor)
  - Append new blocks
  - Validate the entire chain's integrity at any time
 
Theory:
  If block[i].previous_hash == block[i-1].hash for every i,
  and every block's stored hash matches its own computed hash,
  the chain is intact. Altering any block breaks every link after it.
